This document merges the spell material from the B/X Basic and Expert books into one continuous chapter. Basic spellcasting rules are preserved where Expert assumes them, duplicate entries are collapsed, and the spell descriptions are presented as one alphabetical list.

Using This Chapter

Spells are formulas for gathering and directing magical energy. Each spell has its own words and gestures, and only clerics, elves, and magic-users can cast them.

Spells must be prepared before use. When cast, the prepared copy is erased from the caster’s mind. A caster may prepare the same spell more than once if level permits. Clerics pray for spells; magic-users and elves study from spellbooks.

To regain spells, the caster must be well rested and spend about one uninterrupted hour in study or prayer. Magic-users and elves must also have their spellbooks at hand.

The caster must be able to speak and make the required gestures. A bound or gagged spellcaster cannot cast. A caster may do nothing else in the round a spell is cast, and the spell must be declared before initiative. If the caster loses initiative and then takes damage or fails a saving throw before the spell goes off, the spell is interrupted and lost.

If a spell allows a saving throw, a successful save either negates the spell or halves its effect, as noted in the spell. Range is always given in feet. Duration is given in rounds of 10 seconds or turns of 10 minutes. In the wilderness, spell range is read in yards, but area of effect is not.

Some spells are reversible. Clerical reversals are morally significant and should be governed by alignment and the DM’s judgment. Magic-user and elf reversals are prepared as distinct memorized choices. Unless a spell says otherwise, a reversal uses the same range, duration, saving throw, and area as the standard form.

Spells that modify the same value usually do not stack with one another. They do, however, usually stack with magic items.

If a magic-user’s or elf’s spellbook is lost, it must be replaced at a cost of 1000 gp and one week of study per spell level copied. New spells for player-character magic-users and elves are normally learned from a guild, mentor, or apprenticeship during downtime.

Spell Descriptions

Anti-Magic Shell

Magic-user 6
Range: 0' Duration: 12 turns

Creates a personal barrier that blocks all magic, including the caster’s own spells, from passing in or out until the duration ends or the caster drops the shell.

Animate Dead

Magic-user 5
Range: 60' Duration: indefinite

Animates 1 HD of skeletons or zombies per caster level from ordinary corpses or skeletons. Skeletons have AC 7; zombies have AC 8 and one more hit die than the living creature had. Character levels do not count when determining the undead’s hit dice. Animated dead obey the caster until destroyed or dispelled.

Bless*

Cleric 2
Range: 60' Duration: 6 turns

May only be cast on allies not yet in melee. In a 20' × 20' area, it gives +1 morale, +1 to hit, and +1 to damage. It may also serve as part of a blessing or cleansing rite.

Reverse: Blight worsens morale by -1 and imposes -1 to hit and damage unless the targets save vs. Spells.

Charm Monster

Magic-user 4
Range: 120' Duration: special

Works like charm person but affects monsters other than undead. If the targets are 3 HD or less, the caster charms 3d6 hit dice’ worth; otherwise only one monster is affected.

Charm Person

Magic-user 1
Range: 120' Duration: special

Charms one human or human-like creature of ogre size or smaller, excluding undead. A failed save vs. Spells makes the victim regard the caster as a best friend. Commands are usually obeyed unless strongly against the target’s nature, and suicide is refused. New saves are granted over time: daily for high Intelligence, weekly for average Intelligence, monthly for low Intelligence. Dispel magic can break the effect.

Clairvoyance

Magic-user 3
Range: 60' Duration: 12 turns

Lets the caster see through the eyes of one creature in the chosen direction and within range. More than 2' of rock or a thin layer of lead blocks it. Shifting subjects takes a full turn.

Cloudkill

Magic-user 5
Range: 0' Duration: 6 turns

Creates a poisonous cloud 30' across that moves with the wind, or away from the caster if the air is still, at 60' per turn (20' per round). It sinks into hollows and breaks on dense vegetation. Creatures under 5 HD save vs. Poison or die, taking 1 damage on a successful save. Creatures over 5 HD simply take 1 damage per round of exposure.

Commune

Cleric 5
Range: 0' Duration: 3 turns

Allows the cleric to ask the greater powers three yes-or-no questions. It is normally usable once per week, or twice the normal number once per year. The DM may further limit overuse.

Confusion

Magic-user 4
Range: 120' Duration: 12 rounds

Affects 3-18 creatures in a 60' diameter area. Creatures of less than 2+1 HD get no save; stronger creatures save each round. On each affected round, roll 2d6: 2-5 attack the caster’s side, 6-8 do nothing, 9-12 attack their own side.

Conjure Elemental

Magic-user 5
Range: 240' Duration: indefinite

Summons one elemental of the chosen type. The caster must continuously concentrate to control it. If control slips, the elemental turns on the caster and cannot be recontrolled. It serves until slain or dismissed to its home plane. Dispel magic or dispel evil can banish it.

Contact Higher Plane

Magic-user 5
Range: 0' Duration: special

Lets the caster question beings of a higher plane. Higher planes answer more questions, know more, and lie less, but they carry a greater risk of driving the caster insane. The spell is generally usable only once per week, and insanity lasts a number of weeks equal to the plane contacted. The caster chooses the plane before contact.

Continual Light*

Cleric 3; Magic-user 2
Range: 120' Duration: permanent

Creates a 60' sphere of light, bright as full daylight, until dispelled. If cast on a creature’s eyes, the target must save vs. Spells or remain blinded until the spell is dispelled.

Reverse: Continual Darkness creates a 60' sphere of lasting darkness. Even ordinary lights do not shine within it, and infravision does not penetrate it. It and continual light dispel one another.

Control Weather

Magic-user 6
Range: 0' Duration: concentration

Creates one weather effect in a 240-yard radius outdoors: rain, snow, fog, clear weather, intense heat, high winds, or a tornado. Each effect changes vision, movement, missiles, or sea travel as described in the Expert rules. The effect persists only while the caster remains stationary and concentrates.

Create Food

Cleric 5
Range: 0' Duration: permanent

Creates enough food to feed twelve people and their mounts for one day. For every cleric level above 8th, it creates enough for twelve more.

Create Water

Cleric 4
Range: 0' Duration: permanent

Summons an enchanted spring that provides enough water for twelve people and their mounts for one day, about fifty gallons. For each level above 8th, the spell supports twelve more people and mounts.

Cure Disease*

Cleric 3
Range: 30' Duration: permanent

Cures disease, including lycanthropy and mummy rot, and also kills green slime.

Reverse: Cause Disease inflicts a wasting disease unless the victim saves vs. Spells. The victim suffers -2 on all attack rolls; wounds cannot be magically cured; natural healing is halved; and the disease becomes fatal in 2-24 days unless cured.

Cure Light Wounds*

Cleric 1
Range: 0' Duration: permanent

Heals 2-7 points of damage (1d6+1) to a living creature touched by the cleric. It may instead cure paralysis, but then restores no hit points. It cannot raise a creature above its normal maximum.

Reverse: Cause Light Wounds deals 2-7 points of damage to a touched creature on a successful touch attack.

Cure Serious Wounds*

Cleric 4
Range: 0' Duration: permanent

Heals 4-14 points of damage (2d6+2) to one living creature.

Reverse: Cause Serious Wounds deals 4-14 damage on a successful touch attack.

Death Spell

Magic-user 6
Range: 240' Duration: 1 round

Kills 4-32 levels (4d8) of living creatures under 8 HD in a cube 60' on a side. Excess levels are wasted. Each victim may save vs. Death Ray to negate the spell. It has no effect on undead or creatures of 8 HD or more.

Detect Evil

Cleric 1; Magic-user 2
Range: 120' for clerics, 60' for magic-users Duration: 6 turns for clerics, 2 turns for magic-users

Reveals evil intent and evil enchantment by making the target glow. It does not read thoughts. Poison and ordinary traps are neither good nor evil.

Detect Invisible

Magic-user 2
Range: 10' per caster level Duration: 6 turns

Reveals invisible creatures or objects by sight, provided they are within range.

Detect Magic

Cleric 1; Magic-user 1
Range: 60' Duration: 2 turns

Causes magical persons, places, or objects within range to glow. It is especially useful for discovering magic items and magically secured portals.

Dimension Door

Magic-user 4
Range: 10' Duration: 1 round

Instantly transports the caster or one creature up to 360'. If the exact destination is unknown, direction and distance may be specified instead. The spell automatically fails if it would place the subject inside solid matter. Unwilling targets may save vs. Spells.

Disintegrate

Magic-user 6
Range: 60' Duration: instantaneous

Disintegrates one non-magical object or creature: tree, dragon, ship, or 10' section of wall alike. Creatures may save vs. Death Ray to avoid the effect.

Dispel Evil

Cleric 5
Range: 30' Duration: 1 turn

Banished or destroys an enchanted or undead monster that fails a save vs. Spells. A successful save forces it to flee the area instead. The cleric must remain stationary and concentrate. Cast on one creature, it applies -2 to the saving throw. It also frees a creature from a cursed item.

Dispel Magic

Magic-user 3
Range: 120' Duration: permanent

Removes spell effects in a 20' × 20' × 20' area. It does not affect magic items. Spells cast by higher-level casters resist with a 5% failure chance per level difference.

ESP

Magic-user 2
Range: 60' Duration: 12 turns

Lets the caster hear thoughts in one chosen direction after one full turn of concentration. A single creature’s thoughts may be understood regardless of language. Through rock, the limit is 2'; lead blocks it. Undead thoughts cannot be heard.

Feeblemind

Magic-user 5
Range: 240' Duration: indefinite

Reduces a magic-user or elf to helpless idiocy unless the victim makes a save vs. Spells at -4. It has no effect on other classes or most monsters. Dispel magic ends it.

Find Traps

Cleric 2
Range: 30' Duration: 2 turns

Makes trapped areas glow dull blue when the cleric approaches within 30'. It reveals where traps are, but not their exact nature or method of removal.

Fire Ball

Magic-user 3
Range: 240' Duration: instantaneous

Creates a missile that bursts into a fire ball 40' across (20' radius). It deals 1d6 damage per caster level to all creatures in the area, or half on a successful save vs. Spells.

Floating Disc

Magic-user 1
Range: 6' Duration: 6 turns

Creates an invisible floating platform about the size of a small round shield. It carries up to 5000 cn (500 pounds) and follows the caster at waist height so long as the caster remains within 6'; otherwise it closes the gap automatically. At the end of the spell, the disc vanishes and drops its load.

Fly

Magic-user 3
Range: 0' Duration: 1d6 turns + caster level

Allows the caster or one touched subject to fly in any direction at up to 120' per round and hover in place like levitate.

Geas*

Magic-user 6
Range: 30' Duration: special

Compels a creature to perform or avoid a specified action unless it saves vs. Spells. The command must be possible and not directly fatal, or the geas rebounds on the caster. Ignoring a geas brings cumulative penalties until obedience or death.

Reverse: Remove Geas breaks a geas with a 5% failure chance per level the caster is below the one who imposed it.

Growth of Animals

Cleric 3
Range: 120' Duration: 12 turns

Doubles the size of one normal or giant animal. The animal gains double strength, double damage, and double carrying capacity. Intelligence and behavior do not change.

Growth of Plants

Magic-user 4
Range: 120' Duration: special

Causes natural vegetation to thicken into an impassable mass over up to 3000 square feet. It lasts until dispelled and requires existing plant life.

Hallucinatory Terrain

Magic-user 4
Range: 240' Duration: special

Creates the illusion of terrain or conceals a real feature. The whole feature must lie within range. The illusion lasts until touched by an intelligent creature.

Haste

Magic-user 3
Range: 240' Duration: 3 turns

Allows up to 24 creatures in a 60' diameter circle to move at double speed and make double the normal number of missile or melee attacks. It does not speed spellcasting or wand use.

Hold Monster

Magic-user 5
Range: 120' Duration: caster level + 6 turns

As hold person, but it can affect any monster except undead. A single target saves at -2; a group of 1d4 monsters saves normally.

Hold Person

Cleric 2; Magic-user 3
Range: 180' for clerics, 120' for magic-users Duration: 9 turns for clerics, 1 turn per level for magic-users

Affects one human, demi-human, or human-like creature, or 1d4 such creatures if cast at a group. The victims must save vs. Spells or be paralyzed. A single target saves at -2; a group saves normally. It does not affect undead or creatures larger than ogres.

Hold Portal

Magic-user 1
Range: 10' Duration: 2-12 turns

Magically seals a door, gate, or similar portal. Knock opens it. A creature with at least 3 HD more than the caster can break it in one round.

Infravision

Magic-user 3
Range: 0' Duration: 1 day

Lets the subject see heat patterns in darkness to 60'.

Insect Plague

Cleric 5
Range: 480' Duration: 1 day

Creates a 60' diameter swarm that obscures sight and drives off creatures under 3 HD. It moves 20' per round under the caster’s direction while the caster remains stationary and concentrating. If disturbed, the swarm disperses. It only works above ground.

Invisibility

Magic-user 2
Range: 240' Duration: special

Makes the caster or one person or object invisible. Worn and carried items vanish with the subject, but dropped items become visible again. The spell lasts until the subject attacks or casts a spell.

Invisibility 10’ Radius

Magic-user 3
Range: 120' Duration: special

Makes the chosen creature and all creatures within 10' at the time of casting invisible. The field moves with the main subject; companions who drift farther than 10' become visible again. Otherwise it follows the normal invisibility rules.

Invisible Stalker

Magic-user 6
Range: 0' Duration: special

Summons an invisible stalker that follows the caster’s orders until the task is complete, regardless of time or distance. It can be dismissed only by destruction in combat or by clerical dispel evil.

Knock

Magic-user 2
Range: 60' Duration: 1 round

Opens secret doors, held or wizard-locked doors, barred or stuck gates, locked chests, and similar closures.

Know Alignment

Cleric 2
Range: 10' Duration: 1 round

Reveals whether one creature is lawful, neutral, or chaotic. It also reveals the alignment of an enchanted item or place.

Levitate

Magic-user 2
Range: 0' Duration: 6 turns + caster level

Allows vertical movement at 20' per round with no lateral motion except by pushing off surfaces. It affects only the caster, who may carry a normal load.

Light*

Cleric 1; Magic-user 1
Range: 120' Duration: 12 turns for clerics, 6 turns + caster level for magic-users

Creates a sphere of light 30' across, bright enough to read by but not true daylight. It may be cast on an object. If cast into a creature’s eyes, the target may save vs. Spells or be blinded for the duration.

Reverse: Darkness creates a 30' sphere of darkness. Infravision works within it. It cancels light and is canceled by it.

Lightning Bolt

Magic-user 3
Range: 180' Duration: instantaneous

Projects a bolt 5' wide and 60' long. It deals 1d6 damage per caster level, halved on a successful save vs. Spells. If it hits a solid barrier before reaching full length, it rebounds toward the caster until the full distance is spent.

Locate Object

Cleric 3; Magic-user 2
Range: 120' for clerics, 60' + 10' per caster level for magic-users Duration: 6 turns for clerics, 2 turns for magic-users

Finds a specifically known object or common object type and points toward the nearest one within range. It does not locate creatures and does not indicate distance.

Lower Water

Magic-user 6
Range: 240' Duration: 10 turns

Lowers the depth of water in an area of up to 10,000 square feet by 50%.

Magic Jar

Magic-user 5
Range: 30' Duration: special

Places the caster’s life force into a container, leaving the body in trance. From the jar, the caster may attempt to possess creatures within 120'. Failed targets cannot be retried for one turn. A possessed creature uses its own body but not its own spells. Destroying the jar while the life force is inside kills the caster. Destroying the jar while the caster is in another body strands the caster in that body. Dispel evil can force the caster out of a host.

Magic Missile

Magic-user 1
Range: 150' Duration: 1 turn

Creates glowing missiles that automatically hit visible targets, each dealing 2-7 damage (1d6+1). One missile is fired at first; two more are gained for every five caster levels. The missiles may all strike one target or be divided among several.

Massmorph

Magic-user 4
Range: 240' Duration: special

Makes up to 100 human- or man-sized creatures in a 240' diameter area appear to be trees in an orchard or dense wood. The illusion ends when dispelled, when the caster wills it away, or when a disguised creature leaves the original area.

Mirror Image

Magic-user 2
Range: 0' Duration: 6 turns

Creates 1-4 illusory duplicates of the caster. Any attack on the caster strikes an image first, destroying that image whether the attack roll would normally hit or not.

Move Earth

Magic-user 6
Range: 240' Duration: 6 turns

Moves or reshapes earth, but not stone, within range. The spell works down to its depth limit or until solid rock is reached, and shifts earth at 60' per turn.

Neutralize Poison

Cleric 4
Range: 0' Duration: permanent

Cancels poison and revives a poisoned creature if cast within 10 rounds of poisoning. It may also render poison or a poisoned object harmless, but only affects poison present when cast.

Part Water

Magic-user 6
Range: 120' Duration: 6 turns

Opens a path up to 120' long and 10' wide through water so creatures may walk on the bottom. The caster may end it early.

Pass-Wall

Magic-user 5
Range: 30' Duration: 3 turns

Opens a circular passage 5' across and up to 10' deep in rock or stone. It closes when the spell ends.

Phantasmal Force

Magic-user 2
Range: 240' Duration: concentration

Creates an illusion filling up to a 20' × 20' × 20' space. If used as an attack, victims who save vs. Spells are unaffected. If used to create a monster, the creature has AC 9 and vanishes when hit. Illusory deaths, petrifications, and similar effects are not real; victims typically collapse, panic, or become paralyzed for 1-4 turns. If the caster moves or is disturbed in combat, the illusion ends.

Polymorph Others

Magic-user 4
Range: 60' Duration: special

Transforms one living creature into another living creature whose hit dice do not exceed twice the original form’s. The target keeps its hit points but otherwise becomes the new creature, including its instincts and behavior. Unwilling targets save vs. Polymorph. The spell lasts until dispelled or death.

Polymorph Self

Magic-user 4
Range: 0' Duration: caster level + 6 turns

Lets the caster assume the physical form of any living creature of equal or lower hit dice. Hit points, attack rolls, and saving throws do not change. Physical movement and strength may change, but special attacks, spell abilities, and innate immunities do not. The caster cannot cast spells while transformed.

Projected Image

Magic-user 6
Range: 240' Duration: 6 turns

Creates a visual double indistinguishable from the caster except by touch. The caster’s spells appear to originate from the image, though the caster must still see the true target. Melee contact dispels it; missiles and spells seem to pass harmlessly through.

Protection from Evil

Cleric 1; Magic-user 1
Range: 0' Duration: 12 turns for clerics, 6 turns for magic-users

Creates a moving barrier around the caster. Evil attackers suffer -1 to hit the protected subject, while the protected subject gains +1 on saving throws against them. Enchanted or created monsters cannot make melee attacks through it unless the protected subject attacks them in melee first.

Protection from Evil 10’ Radius

Cleric 4; Magic-user 3
Range: 0' Duration: 12 turns

Extends the standard protection from evil barrier to all allies within 10'.

Protection from Normal Missiles

Magic-user 3
Range: 30' Duration: 12 turns

Makes one creature completely immune to small non-magical missiles. It does not stop magical missiles or huge projectiles such as catapult stones.

Purify Food and Water

Cleric 1
Range: 10' Duration: indefinite

Purifies spoiled or poisoned food and water. One casting affects one ration, six waterskins, or enough ordinary food for twelve people.

Quest*

Cleric 5
Range: 30' Duration: special

Compels a target to perform a specific, non-suicidal task unless the target saves vs. Spells. Refusal brings an ongoing curse set by the DM until the quest is resumed or fulfilled.

Reverse: Remove Quest dispels an unwanted quest or quest-related curse, but its chance of success is reduced by 5% per level the caster is below the cleric who imposed the quest.

Raise Dead*

Cleric 5
Range: 120' Duration: permanent

Restores a dead human, dwarf, halfling, or elf to life. An 8th-level cleric may raise one dead up to 4 days; each cleric level above 8th adds 4 more days. The raised character returns with 1 hp, cannot fight, cast, use abilities, carry heavy loads, or move above half speed, and needs 2 full weeks of bed rest to recover. Cast against one undead creature, it slays the undead unless it saves vs. Spells.

Reverse: Finger of Death kills one creature unless it saves vs. Death Ray.

Read Languages

Magic-user 1
Range: 0' Duration: 2 turns

Allows the caster to read unknown languages, ciphers, symbols, and treasure maps for the duration.

Read Magic

Magic-user 1
Range: 0' Duration: 1 turn

Lets the caster read magical writing, runes, and scrolls. Once a particular magical text has been read with this spell, the caster can understand that same text later without recasting read magic.

Reincarnation

Magic-user 6
Range: 0' Duration: permanent

Returns a dead character to life in a new body. The new form may be another character class or a monster, as determined by the Expert reincarnation tables and alignment. If the result is a class, level is rerolled up to the slain character’s former level. If the result is a monster, it cannot have more hit dice than the slain character had level-equivalent, and it does not gain experience as a monster.

Remove Fear*

Cleric 1
Range: 0' Duration: 2 turns

Calms a touched creature and removes fear. A target fleeing from magical fear may save vs. Spells with a bonus equal to the cleric’s level to shake it off.

Reverse: Cause Fear forces one creature to save vs. Spells or flee for 2 turns. Range 120'.

Remove Curse*

Cleric 3; Magic-user 4
Range: 0' Duration: permanent

Removes one curse from a creature or cursed magical item.

Reverse: Curse imposes a chosen misfortune, such as attack penalties, saving-throw penalties, or a reduced prime requisite. A successful saving throw negates it. Overpowering curses may rebound on the caster at the DM’s discretion.

Resist Cold

Cleric 1
Range: 30' Duration: 6 turns

Protects creatures in range from ordinary freezing conditions. It grants +2 on saving throws against magical cold and reduces cold damage by 1 per die, never below 1 per die.

Resist Fire

Cleric 2
Range: 30' Duration: 2 turns

Protects one creature from ordinary fire and heat. It also grants +2 on saves against magical fire and reduces such damage by 1 per die, never below 1 per die.

Shield

Magic-user 1
Range: 0' Duration: 2 turns

Creates a moving magical shield that grants AC 2 against missiles and AC 4 against all other attacks.

Silence 15’ Radius

Cleric 2
Range: 180' Duration: 12 turns

Creates a 30' diameter zone of perfect silence. Speech and spellcasting within it are impossible, though outside noises can still be heard. If cast on a creature, the victim may save vs. Spells to keep the effect fixed in place rather than moving with them.

Sleep

Magic-user 1
Range: 240' Duration: 4-16 turns

Puts creatures of 4+1 HD or less to sleep. One 4+1 HD creature may be affected, or 2-16 HD of weaker creatures. Undead are immune. Lower-HD creatures are affected first. Sleeping targets can be awakened by force or slain with one edged-weapon blow.

Snake Charm

Cleric 2
Range: 60' Duration: special

Charms 1 HD of snakes per cleric level. The snakes sway harmlessly instead of attacking. Against attacking snakes, duration is 2-5 rounds (1d4+1); otherwise it is 2-5 turns.

Speak with Animals

Cleric 2
Range: 30' Duration: 6 turns

Allows the cleric to converse with one kind of normal or giant animal. Fantastic creatures and intelligent animal races are unaffected. Favorable animal reaction may allow a simple service or task.

Speak with Plants

Cleric 4
Range: 30' Duration: 3 turns

Allows the cleric to converse with plants and ask simple favors within their power. It can also enable passage through undergrowth and communication with plantlike monsters such as treants.

Stone to Flesh*

Magic-user 6
Range: 120' Duration: permanent

Turns stone to flesh, most often restoring petrified creatures.

Reverse: Flesh to Stone turns one living creature, along with all gear, to stone unless the victim saves vs. Turn to Stone.

Sticks to Snakes

Cleric 4
Range: 120' Duration: 6 turns

Turns 2-16 sticks into snakes (AC 6, HD 1, move 90' (30'), 1 attack for 1-4 damage). There is a 50% chance they are poisonous. They obey the cleric until the spell ends or they are slain.

Striking

Cleric 3
Range: 30' Duration: 1 turn

Enchants one ordinary weapon to deal an extra 1d6 damage per hit, as with a staff of striking. It does not improve the chance to hit, but it does allow the weapon to harm creatures needing magical weapons.

Telekinesis

Magic-user 5
Range: 120' Duration: 6 rounds

Moves up to 200 cn per caster level at 20' per round in any direction. Unwilling creatures may save vs. Spells. The caster must concentrate; disturbed objects fall.

Teleport

Magic-user 5
Range: 10' Duration: instantaneous

Instantly transports the caster or another creature to any known location the caster can visualize. Success depends on how well the destination is known: casual knowledge is risky, general knowledge is safer, exact knowledge safest. A bad result may place the subject high above the point, too low inside solid matter, or exactly on target. Unwilling targets may save vs. Spells.

Transmute Rock to Mud*

Magic-user 5
Range: 120' Duration: 3-18 days

Turns up to 3000 square feet of rock, 10' deep, into deep mud. Creatures in it move at one-tenth normal speed.

Reverse: Transmute Mud to Rock permanently hardens the same volume of mud into stone.

Ventriloquism

Magic-user 1
Range: 60' Duration: 2 turns

Projects the caster’s voice so that it seems to come from another source, such as a statue or animal.

Wall of Fire

Magic-user 4
Range: 60' Duration: special

Creates up to 1200 square feet of opaque fire in any supported shape. Creatures under 4 HD cannot cross. Stronger creatures may cross, taking 1d6 damage, or 2d6 if undead or cold-using. The wall lasts only while the caster stands still and concentrates.

Wall of Ice

Magic-user 4
Range: 120' Duration: 12 turns

Creates a supported translucent wall 20' high and 60' long, or equivalent shape. Creatures under 4 HD cannot break through. Stronger creatures take 1d6 damage, or 2d6 if fire-using, when they do.

Wall of Stone

Magic-user 5
Range: 60' Duration: special

Creates a supported stone wall 2' thick and up to 1000 cubic feet in any shape. It lasts until broken or dispelled.

Water Breathing

Magic-user 3
Range: 30' Duration: 1 day

Allows the subject to breathe underwater at any depth. It does not improve swimming or air-breathing.

Web

Magic-user 2
Range: 10' Duration: 48 turns

Fills a 10' × 10' × 10' area with sticky strands. Giants or similarly strong creatures break through in 2 rounds; ordinary humans need 2-8 turns. Fire destroys the web in 2 rounds but burns anything trapped inside for 1-6 damage.

Wizard Eye

Magic-user 4
Range: 240' Duration: 6 turns

Creates an invisible eye with infravision 60' that can move 120' per turn, so long as it remains within 240' of the caster. The caster must concentrate to see through it.

Wizard Lock

Magic-user 2
Range: 10' Duration: permanent

Permanently locks a door or locked object. Knock or the original caster can open it without dispelling it. A magic-user 3 or more levels above the original caster can pass a wizard-locked portal without magic.
